# What practitioners say is missing

> Snapshot: 2026-10-17. Collected from issue trackers of popular RAG engines and from Hacker News discussions in 2025–2026. Issue numbers are examples, not a complete count.

| # | Gap | Evidence | Where we answer it |
|---|-----|----------|--------------------|
| 1 | **Deleting and updating documents is unreliable.** Deleted files keep being cited; incremental sync misses deletions; changing the embedding model means wiping the index. | RAGFlow keeps citing deleted files ([#9503](https://github.com/infiniflow/ragflow/issues/9503), [#9578](https://github.com/infiniflow/ragflow/issues/9578), [#9883](https://github.com/infiniflow/ragflow/issues/9883)) and misses deletions in delta sync ([#13708](https://github.com/infiniflow/ragflow/issues/13708)). LightRAG deletion and cache-cleanup bugs ([#985](https://github.com/HKUDS/LightRAG/issues/985), [#3833](https://github.com/HKUDS/LightRAG/issues/3833)). Dify storage not freed after deletion ([#37066](https://github.com/langgenius/dify/issues/37066)) and upgrades breaking knowledge bases ([#27291](https://github.com/langgenius/dify/issues/27291)). | Tombstone-then-purge deletion (FR-ING-11, QR-PRIV-04); immutable versions (I-02); [index generations](../adr/0004-index-generations.md) |
| 2 | **Parsing tables, charts and scans is the hardest part, and expensive.** | Tables and multi-page tables called "the hardest part" ([HN](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46085800)). | 8 | **Little observability, too many knobs.** | Requests for tracing in LightRAG ([#2936](https://github.com/HKUDS/LightRAG/issues/2936)); timeout tuning ([#1630](https://github.com/HKUDS/LightRAG/issues/1630)); Dify retrieval failing beyond 100 files ([#29750](https://github.com/langgenius/dify/issues/29750)). | Traces for every query and job (FR-OBS-01), a validated configuration with startup checks (FR-ADM-01) |

## The opportunity

The gaps that come up most often are the ones open-rag-engine is designed around: **deletion-correct incremental indexing, page-exact citations that are verified, an evaluation loop that runs on a CPU, a minimal footprint, and GraphRAG that degrades gracefully on small local models.**
